1. What analytical questions the Gold layer should answer?

A. Culture and susceptibility overview
- How many culture orders, organisms, and antibiotic-result observations are represented?
- What are the distributions of observed susceptibility categories?
- How many observations have unavailable or uninterpreted results?

B. Antimicrobial resistance history
- How are recorded resistance-history observations distributed by organism and antibiotic?
- How do recorded resistance-history intervals vary across organisms and antibiotics?

C. Prior antibiotic exposure
- Which antibiotic classes and subtypes appear most frequently in prior-exposure records?
- How do exposure records vary by their recorded interval relative to a culture?

D. Prior clinical history
- Which prior organisms, medications, and procedures are most frequently recorded?
- How do these records vary across culture orders?
